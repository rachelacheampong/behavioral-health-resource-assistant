# Behavioral Health Resource Assistant

A Claude-powered decision-support project designed to help behavioral health professionals identify relevant community resources and generate clear, consistent referral summaries.

## Project Overview

Behavioral health staff often rely on resource guides, manual searches, and institutional knowledge to connect clients with appropriate services. This process can be time-consuming and may lead to inconsistent referrals, especially when staff must quickly assess several client needs at once.

The Behavioral Health Resource Assistant explores how Claude can support this workflow by interpreting natural-language requests, identifying relevant resources from an approved source list, explaining why each resource may fit, and generating a concise referral summary.

The assistant is designed to support, not replace, professional judgment.

## The Problem

Community behavioral health resources are often distributed across multiple guides and categories, including mental health services, substance use treatment, crisis support, housing, food assistance, and recovery services.

Staff may need to quickly determine:

- Which resources match a client’s age and needs
- Whether crisis support should be prioritized
- Which organizations provide specific services
- What follow-up questions should be asked
- How to summarize a referral clearly and consistently

This project demonstrates how generative AI may help organize existing information and reduce the time required to navigate available resources.

## Core Functions

The assistant is designed to:

- Match client needs with relevant resources
- Ask clarifying questions when important information is missing
- Explain why a resource may be appropriate
- Compare available resource options
- Generate concise referral summaries
- Clearly identify when human review is required

## Responsible AI Approach

The assistant is not intended to:

- Diagnose a mental health or substance use condition
- Provide medical or clinical advice
- Determine eligibility when it is not stated in the source material
- Confirm service availability
- Replace crisis professionals or behavioral health staff
- Invent resource information

Recommendations must remain grounded in the approved resource information provided to Claude.
